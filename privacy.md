# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026.

Invoice Maker is developed by Rodriguez Kayembe Mukala. This policy describes the iPhone app's handling of information.

## Information you enter

The app stores the business details, customer details, services, invoices, quotes, payment records, notes, logos, signature images, and stamp images that you choose to enter. This information is used to create documents, display their history, and provide the functions you request. You do not need to create a separate Invoice Maker account.

The app stores this information locally on your device. If you use iCloud, the app synchronizes it through a private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Apple operates iCloud and handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not operate a server that receives your invoice database.

## Photos, signatures, and contacts

The system photo picker provides only the images you select for a logo or stamp. Drawn signatures are stored as images for inclusion in your PDFs; the app does not provide identity verification or a certified signing service. The system contact picker supplies only the contact you choose to import. The app does not upload your entire photo library or address book to the developer.

## Purchases

Apple processes subscription payments. The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework to verify access to subscription features. The developer does not receive your card number or billing credentials. Apple may provide transaction and sales information to the developer under its App Store services.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local invoice reminders. It does not send your invoice contents to a developer-operated notification server.

## Sharing and backups

PDFs, exports, and full backup archives are shared only when you use the system sharing or Files controls. The destination you choose may receive the information in those files and handles it under its own policies. Backup archives may contain sensitive business and customer data; store and share them carefully. The app does not encrypt manually exported backup archives.

## Analytics and advertising

The app does not include third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. The developer does not sell your information. Apple may collect diagnostics and purchase-related information according to your device settings and Apple's policies. GitHub hosts this website and may process visitor information under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Retention and control

Your app documents remain stored locally and, when enabled, in your private iCloud database until you remove them. Issued documents are protected from editing and deletion inside the app. Keep any records required for your business before removing the app or its iCloud data. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete iCloud data or backups that you exported. Manage iCloud storage through your Apple Account settings and delete exported files separately. Cancelling a subscription does not delete your documents.

## Questions and requests

Contact the developer through [the public support repository](https://github.com/Rodriguez7/invoice-maker/issues/new). Do not post personal information, invoices, tax IDs, bank details, signatures, or receipts in a public issue. Request a private follow-up when necessary. For information held only on your device or in your Apple Account, the developer cannot inspect or retrieve your records; you control those copies through the app, Files, and Apple settings.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
