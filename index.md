# Invoice Maker

Native invoicing for iPhone, with local storage and optional private iCloud sync.

## Help

- **Create an invoice:** complete your business profile, add a customer and services, review the PDF, then issue the invoice. Issuing requires an active subscription.
- **Correct a mistake:** edit a draft. For an issued invoice, create a cancellation document and issue a replacement.
- **Record a payment:** open an issued invoice and choose Record payment. Payments are recorded manually; the app does not collect card payments from your customers.
- **iCloud:** sign in to your Apple Account and enable iCloud for the app. Keep a separate backup: sync can propagate deletions.
- **Back up:** Settings → Yearly tax export & full backup → Export full backup archive. Save it somewhere private. Archives can contain names, addresses, payment details, signatures, and invoice PDFs.
- **Subscription:** purchases are billed by Apple. Use Restore purchases to recover an active subscription. Manage or cancel through [Apple subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions). Existing documents and exports remain accessible after subscription access ends.

## Contact support

[Open a support issue](https://github.com/Rodriguez7/invoice-maker/issues/new) with the app version, iOS version, and a description of the problem. **Issues are public. Do not attach invoices, customer information, tax IDs, bank details, signatures, backup archives, or purchase receipts.** Ask for a private follow-up if sensitive information is needed.

Read the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) and [Apple Standard Terms of Use](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).

Country presets help prepare documents; users remain responsible for checking that their invoices meet the requirements applicable to their business.
